### Strategic priorities supported by current evidence

1. **Conversion quality:** Sessions grew 38.2% while confirmed bookings grew 0.5%. Review conversion by device, service, landing page, and booking flow. Distinguish website measurement issues from real guest drop-off.
2. **Protect premium treatments:** Wix AOV grew 19.9%, with deep tissue, couples, and mobile offers contributing significant Wix sale-item value. Evaluate **net contribution per completed treatment** before promoting lower-priced alternatives.
3. **Partner distribution discipline:** Trace bookings from villas, hotels, concierges, and regional partners to cash collected and therapist settlement. Website traffic alone does not measure profitable partner distribution.

- November 2026 and Q4 2026 are **partial**. The 2025 comparison is matched exactly through November 17, not all of November.
- Website unique visitors and CRM customers are not summed across the two sites as unique persons.
- The public GitHub reporting repository contains aggregated data only; no private guest identifiers, individual payment details, partner PII, or secrets.
- The full 2024 management report, relevant accounting ledgers, therapist payouts, operating expenses and payment processor statements must be reconciled before using these figures in a financial statement claiming audited/verified profitability.
- Caribbean operating tracker, confirmed partner pricing, and independent website/GA4 channel data remain separate sections with their own source controls. Do not infer Caribbean collected revenue from those tracker totals.
