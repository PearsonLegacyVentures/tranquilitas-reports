That argues for investigating funnel conversion and source quality, while protecting high-ticket positioning. It does not by itself prove changes in customer acquisition costs or profitability.

### Priorities to investigate
1. **Booking conversion integrity:** Sessions rose 49.2% versus same-period 2025 without an equivalent increase in confirmed Wix bookings. Examine landing pages, device mix, booking-path failures, and attribution tracking.
2. **Margin by service:** Use therapist payouts, transport, supplies, payment fees, and referral commissions to evaluate the true contribution of couples, deep tissue, mobile and beach treatments before scaling offers.
3. **Tourism distribution:** Test Airbnb/villa concierge and luxury hospitality channels with tracked referrals and contract terms, optimizing completed profitable bookings, not just referral volume.

### Source and quality limitations
- **Origin:** Wix Analytics Semantic Models API, two Tranquilitas-owned sites. Historical monthly records, true quarterly/annual aggregates, annual channel/service/SEO breakdowns. Extract through Oct 28, 2026.
- **Data sets:** Traffic, sales, bookings, service sales, button clicks, conversion, Google Search, CRM contact aggregates, transactions, and internal site-search (no observations).
- **Revenue ≠ cash:** The uploaded 2024 report contains unreconciled annual-vs-quarter income differences. Wix reported sales amount differs dramatically from separately reported paid amounts on some historical periods. Do not call either an audited financial number without reconciliation against accounting, bank and processor statements.
- **Partial year:** 2026 is YTD through Oct 28. Q4 2026 is a partial quarter; comparisons with full prior Q4s are not apples-to-apples.
- **No identity data:** Customer names, contact details, booking identifiers, individual transactions and confidential financial details were not exported to this repository.
- **Deployment caution:** The GitHub repository is public. The data it contains are aggregated only. A true confidential management portal would require private hosting/data access controls.
