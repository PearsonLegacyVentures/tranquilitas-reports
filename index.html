07 / DATA GOVERNANCE

Source register & caveats

Essential context for decision quality
Financial reporting boundary. Wix “sales amount” represents reported commerce activity; it does not equal bank cash received, audited turnover, gross profit, or net income. Wix’s separately reported “paid” fields are much lower in some periods, likely reflecting payment-recording methods; they must be reconciled against payment processors and accounting records before any statutory or investor financial statements. All customer-level records remain excluded from this public repository.
